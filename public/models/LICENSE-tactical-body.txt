The creator's publication explicitly licenses the model and diffuse texture
under CC0. It is an original, manually modeled, rigged human, not a MakeHuman
export. The author describes the public version as safe for work and
non-identifying.

Deadlight Ops normalizes the body to grounded game units, maps its 39-joint rig,
uses one creator-authored subdivision level for the near mesh and the base mesh
for distant detail, retains UVs and normalized skin weights, and converts the
packed 1024px diffuse texture to JPEG. The winter clothing, goggles, mask,
helmet, equipment, scoped rifles and runtime poses are Deadlight Ops additions.
Preparation: scripts/prepare-realistic-body.py, run offline in Blender with
factory settings and automatic script execution disabled.

Neither the previous Quaternius superhero base nor the former Mixamo/Three.js
example model or animations are used by these prepared assets.
